Frequently Asked Questions

Can I connect my own database?
Yes! You can connect any external PostgreSQL, MySQL, or SQL Server database by providing the connection credentials. Alternatively, use our hosted database service with zero configuration.
How does the identity provider work?
We provide a fully managed identity provider built into the platform. You can create users, configure OAuth/OIDC protocols, and protect your APIs — all without managing external auth infrastructure. You can also connect your own external IdP.
What happens after the free trial?
After 14 days, you can choose to upgrade to Pro or Enterprise. Your workspace and data remain intact — no disruption. If you don't upgrade, your account moves to a read-only state.
Can I deploy custom API endpoints?
Absolutely. While we auto-generate CRUD endpoints for every table, you can also create and deploy custom API endpoints with your own business logic.
