Frequently Asked Questions

What happens after the free trial?

After 14 days, you can upgrade to Pro or Enterprise. Your data stays intact. If you don't upgrade, your account becomes read-only until you choose a plan.

Can I connect my own database?

Yes! Pro and Enterprise plans support external databases. Connect any PostgreSQL, MySQL, or SQL Server by providing credentials.

Can I change plans later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes take effect immediately and billing is prorated.

Do you offer a money-back guarantee?

Yes, we offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans. No questions asked.
